About Me

I'm Gauri, a Philosophy, Politics and Economics (PPE) undergraduate at the London School of Economics (LSE) with a particular interest in corporate finance, markets and investing.

Alongside my studies, I have taken part in finance, consulting and real-estate case competitions and enjoy working on independent projects to deepen my understanding. At LSE, I am an executive committee member of the LSESU M&A Group and a Research Analyst for the LSESU Private Equity Society.

Outside of academics, I enjoy learning languages and have completed a B2 qualification in Spanish and am working towards my Chinese HSK 3 exam. I also enjoy ice-skating and practising the guitar.
